About Us

We believe music belongs to everyone, and that sharing it is one of the most meaningful things we can do.

Heartstrings at Berkeley brings live music to retirement home senior residents, hospital patients, and community members who could use a little brightness in their day. We also create a warm, low-pressure space for students to keep playing, find musical friends, and express themselves, whatever that looks like for you.

Research shows music can ease anxiety, spark long-lost memories, and lift spirits, even for those who can no longer find the words.
