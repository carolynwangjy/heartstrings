Get Involved

Whether you play an instrument, sing in the shower, or just love being around music — you belong here. As a Blue Heart Initiative Berkeley club, there are no auditions, no interviews, and no prerequisites to joining.

Who Can Join

♪ Musicians & singers
  • Performers — Got something you want to share? Bring your instrument and we'll help you find people to play with or build out a solo set. There's a brief screening to make sure our venue performances are a wonderful experience for everyone.
  • Just for fun — Want to play for the joy of it? Come jam with us. No pressure to perform anywhere — just good music and good company.
♡ Non-musicians

You don't need an instrument to be a meaningful part of Heartstrings.

Help us document performances through photography, run our social media, cheer on performers, or simply show up and be part of a community that believes in music's power to heal.

How Meetings Work

Every Friday · 6–8 PM · Room TBD

Kickoff
Welcome & Updates

We kick things off with a quick check-in — upcoming events, how everyone is doing, and a piece of music-wellness research we've been excited about.

Main Session
Free Practice & Jam

The heart of our meetings. Bring your instrument, form casual ensembles, and practice together for our performances — or just sit back and listen. Just jamming and just music.

Wrap-Up
Exec Check-ins

Grab a few minutes with one of our exec members — a low-key chat to see how things are going. If you're thinking about performing, this is a great time to share your ideas and show us what you've been working on.
