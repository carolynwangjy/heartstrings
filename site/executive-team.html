Our Team

Heartstrings was founded by two students, Carolyn P. and Greta, united by a belief that music has the power to heal. Since then, our executive team has grown to welcome new leaders who share that same commitment to music and service.

Carolyn Pyun

Founder & President

Applied Mathematics

Hi! I'm Carolyn. I've been playing violin for 9 years, and I currently perform with the UC Berkeley Symphony Orchestra. My music taste is all over the place. I'll go from Tchaikovsky to Olivia Dean in a second. I'm also learning to play fingerstyle guitar so hmu if you have any tips. Performing in service settings has been a huge part of my life since middle school, and I founded Heartstrings because it was the club I wished existed at Berkeley: a chill space for musicians who love performing and want to give back to the community while doing it.

Why I'm passionate about music as wellness

I see music as an amazing, universal way to connect with people, regardless of their background. Throughout my life, music has played a significant role in my own wellness journey.

Greta Chan

I've been volunteering since my sophomore year of high school, working across the Bay Area in leadership roles, from organizing events and outreach, to supporting children and seniors through tutoring, health education, and community programs. Whether I'm planning initiatives from the ground up or coordinating on site, my goal has always been simple: to help people and the causes they care about.

Why I'm passionate about music as wellness

Alongside service, music has always been my constant. I'm a singer with experience in orchestra, violin, piano, and dance, and you'll almost always find me with headphones on. Heartstrings Club is my way of blending compassion, creativity, and connection, to give back, build community, and let music speak where words sometimes can't. 💛🎶
